Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers for you.

How do I list an item for sale on EduMarket? ▼
Simply create a free account or log in, click the "Sell an Item" button, fill in your product details (photos, price, description, and your campus), and your listing is live immediately!
Is EduMarket free to use for students? ▼
Yes! EduMarket is completely free for student buyers and sellers to list, browse, and connect directly on campus.
How do payments and item handoffs work? ▼
Buyers and sellers chat directly on WhatsApp or phone to agree on a public meetup location on campus (e.g. university library or student lounge) to inspect the item and complete payment via Mobile Money (OM/MOMO) or cash.
